*Sample editorial content for a fictional business website.*

A growing business collects information from many places: invoices, receipts, order systems and shared spreadsheets. Consistent categories help people read those records without guessing.

## Start with familiar language

Choose labels that your team understands. In this fictional example, a small shop separates supplies, equipment and delivery costs so each group is easy to review.

## Keep a short reference

A shared note can explain what belongs in each category and who answers questions. That gives new team members a starting point and keeps decisions from living in one person's memory.

## Review the exceptions

When an item does not fit, flag it for review instead of creating another nearly identical label. A short conversation can prevent a long list of inconsistent entries.

## Build a repeatable habit

Make record review part of the regular workflow. The goal is a system that people can maintain comfortably, with fewer surprises when they need to find something later.
