*Sample editorial content for a fictional business website.*

Working with a bookkeeping partner involves more than sending a folder of documents. A thoughtful handoff gives both sides a shared picture of the work.

## Describe the current routine

List the systems you use, the records you maintain and the people who know where things live. This helps the partner understand your workflow before suggesting changes.

## Agree on the scope

Write down which tasks each person handles, what information is needed and how questions will be raised. Clear expectations reduce confusion during busy periods.

## Keep access organized

Use an agreed process for sharing documents and managing access. Avoid scattering sensitive records across informal conversations.

## Plan the first review

Set a point to discuss what is working and what needs adjustment. A good working relationship leaves room to refine the process together.
