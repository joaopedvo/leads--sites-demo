*Sample editorial content for a fictional business website.*

A report is easier to use when the team knows what conversation it supports. This fictional business sets aside a regular time to discuss its upcoming workload, outstanding invoices and planned purchases.

## Start with a few questions

What has changed since the last review? What information is missing? Which decisions need attention? A short agenda helps keep the discussion focused.

## Show the assumptions

A projection is a working view of what might happen. Make the assumptions visible so readers can tell which items are confirmed and which still need an update.

## Use a familiar format

A consistent layout lets people spend more time discussing the content and less time finding the relevant section. Clear labels and a visible update date can help.

## Record the next steps

End the meeting with a short list of actions and owners. The next review can begin with those notes, creating continuity from one conversation to the next.
